About TrackGuard

TrackGuard is a vehicle tracker installation scheduling platform built to make GPS-tracker installation simple, transparent, and reliable for vehicle owners.

Built Around Trust

We connect vehicle owners with dependable tracker installation, clear appointment details, and support they can count on.

Why GPS tracking matters

A professionally installed tracker can support vehicle recovery, improve visibility, and give owners useful information when it matters.

Our Mission

To give every vehicle owner peace of mind through fast, professionally-installed GPS tracking and an effortless online booking experience.
