About TrackGuard

TrackGuard is a vehicle tracker installation scheduling platform built to make GPS-tracker installation simple, transparent, and reliable for vehicle owners.

Our Mission

How It Works

Create an account, choose an installation service, pick an available date and time slot, add your vehicle details, and confirm — TrackGuard handles the rest.
